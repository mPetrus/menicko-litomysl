# Menu page (site/index.html)

Scope: the whole site, one page. Visitor mode: Operate (scan today's lunch, pick a place, find a dish).
Audience and job: anyone in Litomyšl choosing lunch, phone and monitor equally. Must keep search, day switching, favourite hearts with favourites first. Must not show fewer dishes per screen than today.
Success: feels like Litomyšl, not a template.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The menu printed like a Josef Váchal woodcut (his Portmoneum house is in Litomyšl): heavy black cut line, colour hand-washed inside it. Refuses the category default of soft white cards on a cream page with a polite serif.

OWN-WORLD: Saturated ochre ground (Portmoneum wall), paper-white menu blocks inside heavy black uneven frames, restaurant names cut white out of a black block in Grenze. Colour only inside black outlines: soup rows washed madder red, dessert rows washed green, the selected day solid red. Text always black on paper. States are stamps: a stale or failed menu gets a red rotated stamp. A favourite is ringed in red. Dark mode: black-ink ground, the paper becomes dark ochre-brown, washes stay.

STORY: The visitor sees today's menus at once, scans dish and price, switches day or searches, hearts favourites, follows a name to the restaurant.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Black woodcut band across the top with the cut title "Meníčko Litomyšl", update time and search on one row (desktop); below it the day labels, selected day red. Then menus in columns (3 desktop, 2 tablet, 1 phone), each a framed paper block headed by its black name block with the heart; prices right, tabular. Closed restaurants as a quiet row of small framed labels below.

FORM: woodcut-and-hand-colouring, my grounded list position 4 (assigned), seed:483f7f294b92d66c. Signature move: the restaurant name cut out of a black block; motion grammar: the day switch is a print being pulled (new menus press in with a short downward settle), no fades elsewhere.
